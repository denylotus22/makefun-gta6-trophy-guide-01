An offline, open-source guide and tracker for all 55 Grand Theft Auto VI PlayStation 5 trophies — a checklist with progress saved in the browser, export/import, filters by grade and kind, a spoiler setting that hides story trophies until earned, a six-step roadmap to the platinum, a missables page, and a cited guide for every trophy in our own words. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Build the best GTA 6 trophy guide" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network needed. Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a JSON file and imported back.

## What's inside

- **Tracker** — all 55 official trophies with their PlayStation names, descriptions and grades (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze), a checklist, search, and filters by grade, kind and earned status.
- **Spoiler setting** — hides the names and descriptions of the 12 story trophies until you earn them.
- **Roadmap to the platinum** — six steps built from the official descriptions: story first (~60–80 hours), strangers, activities, collectibles, crime and money, then the 100% checklist. About 200 hours in total, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx).
- **Missables** — only one trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude, whose first meditation spot at Jason's house in Chapter 1 vanishes after that chapter. Everything else is doable in free-roam after the story.
- **Guides** — every trophy has a guide in our own words with its sources named (Exophase for the official list, PSNProfiles for PSN counts, PowerPyx for the roadmap and missable flag). Where a location or trigger is not pinned down yet, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing it.
